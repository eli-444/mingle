A modern Omegle alternative for random video chat

If you miss the simplicity of meeting a random person online, MingleTV offers an independent alternative built around one-to-one video and text conversations for adults. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Omegle.

Why people look for an Omegle alternative

Omegle popularized the idea of instantly talking to a stranger without building a social profile first. After the original service closed, people continued searching for ways to have the same kind of spontaneous conversation. A useful alternative should preserve the simplicity of random matching while making its own rules, privacy choices and safety tools clear.

What MingleTV keeps simple

MingleTV focuses on the core random-chat flow: confirm that you are an adult, allow browser permissions, start searching and talk to the person you are matched with. If you want a different conversation, you can stop and search again.

  • Random one-to-one matching
  • Live video and text chat
  • No public sign-up profile before starting
  • A report button during active conversations
  • Community rules that prohibit sexual content, harassment, hate, threats, scams and illegal content

MingleTV is not Omegle

MingleTV is a separate service with its own operator, technical infrastructure, Terms of Use, Community Rules and Privacy Policy. References to Omegle on this page describe the type of random-chat experience people may be searching for; they do not imply any partnership or affiliation.

What to check before choosing any random chat site

Do not judge an alternative only by how quickly it finds a match. Read its age rules, privacy policy and moderation process. Check whether you can leave immediately, whether reporting is available and what technical information may be processed during a connection.

Using random chat responsibly

Random video chat involves real strangers, so keep personal information private, do not send money and leave if a conversation becomes uncomfortable. MingleTV is adults-only and every user is expected to follow the Community Rules.

Did you mean “Omegle”?

“Omgle” and “omegl” are common misspellings of Omegle. If you arrived here after typing one of those terms and were looking for an Omegle-style random video chat, MingleTV is an independent alternative for adults.
