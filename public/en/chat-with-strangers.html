Chat with strangers online by video or text

MingleTV lets adults talk to someone new without first adding contacts or building a public social profile. Start a random conversation in the browser, talk by live video or text and leave whenever you choose.

What does “chat with strangers” mean?

It means meeting a person you do not already know through a temporary online conversation. Unlike a friends list or direct-message inbox, random chat introduces you to another available user. The interaction can be brief or continue for as long as both people want to stay connected.

Video and text in one place

Not every conversation needs to happen only through the camera. MingleTV also includes a live text area during the session, so participants can type while using video. This can be useful when audio is unclear or when you want to share a short message without leaving the chat.

No profile browsing before you start

MingleTV does not require you to browse cards, follow accounts or wait for someone to accept a friend request. The service is built around immediate, random matching between available adults.

Keep stranger chat private

A stranger does not need your full name, home address, workplace, passwords, banking information or precise location. Be especially careful with requests to move quickly to another platform, send money or reveal identifying information. You can hide your displayed country in MingleTV privacy settings.

When to end a conversation

You never need to stay in a conversation that feels uncomfortable. Stop the chat if the other person is abusive, sexual, threatening, manipulative or simply not someone you want to continue talking to. Use the report control when behaviour violates the rules.
