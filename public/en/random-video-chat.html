Random video chat with strangers online

Random video chat is a simple way to meet someone you would not normally cross paths with. MingleTV pairs available adults for a live one-to-one conversation in the browser, with video and text in the same session.

What is random video chat?

A random video chat connects you with another available person without asking you to browse profiles first. Instead of choosing a contact from a list, you start a search and the service creates a temporary conversation with a stranger. On MingleTV, you can talk by camera and microphone, type messages and leave whenever you want.

How MingleTV works

MingleTV runs in a modern web browser and uses real-time communication technology to establish conversations. You remain in control of camera and microphone permissions through the browser. Once you confirm that you are an adult, you can start searching for another available user.

  • No public profile or traditional registration form is required before starting.
  • Live video and text chat are available in the same conversation.
  • You can stop a conversation and search for someone new.
  • You can hide your displayed country and, when available, choose relay-only connectivity in privacy settings.

Random chat without endless profiles

Some social platforms are built around profiles, followers and recommendation feeds. Random chat is different: the point is the conversation itself. MingleTV is designed for people who want a direct, spontaneous interaction rather than a long onboarding process.

Safety matters when talking to strangers

A random conversation can be unpredictable. Do not share passwords, financial information, your exact address or other details that could put you at risk. MingleTV is restricted to adults and prohibits nudity, sexual content, harassment, hate, threats, scams and illegal content. If someone breaks the rules, leave the conversation and use the report control.

Random video chat on desktop and mobile

Because MingleTV is browser based, there is no separate desktop program required. A compatible browser, internet connection, camera and microphone are enough to start. Performance still depends on your device, browser permissions and network quality.
