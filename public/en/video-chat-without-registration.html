Random video chat without registration

MingleTV removes the public sign-up step from random video chat. You do not need to create a profile, choose a public username or submit an email address through a registration form before entering the chat.

What “without registration” means on MingleTV

There is no traditional public account-creation flow before you start. MingleTV may still create short-lived technical session identifiers that are necessary to operate the service securely. The Privacy Policy explains what is processed and why.

Why browser-based chat is convenient

A browser-based service avoids a separate installer. You can open MingleTV on a compatible device, grant camera and microphone access when you choose and start searching. Your browser remains responsible for permission prompts.

No registration does not mean no rules

MingleTV is restricted to adults and the Community Rules still apply to every conversation. Nudity, sexual content, harassment, hate, threats, scams, illegal content and attempts to bypass service restrictions are prohibited.

Privacy choices during random chat

You can choose whether your estimated country is shown to your partner. When relay functionality is available, privacy settings can also offer relay-only connectivity to avoid a direct peer connection. The Privacy Policy describes the limits and data processing involved.

What you should still protect yourself

Even without a public profile, the person on the other side can see or hear whatever you intentionally transmit. Avoid showing identifying documents, private addresses, financial information or backgrounds that reveal where you live. Assume another participant could record their own screen even though MingleTV does not automatically attach video recordings to reports.
